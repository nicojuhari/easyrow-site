EasyRow

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

EasyRow opens CSV and Excel files on your phone and shows them as cards. Your spreadsheets are read on your device. They are never uploaded to us, and we don’t collect, sell or share your personal data.

Who we are

EasyRow (“the app”) is developed by Nicolae Cojuhari. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@nicojuhari.com.

Your files stay on your device

  • When you import a file, the app reads it on your phone. We have no servers that receive your spreadsheets.
  • To open a file, the app keeps a working copy in its private storage on your device. If you edit rows, the edited copy (and any pictures from the file) is also saved there.
  • A list of recently opened files (names, row counts and when you opened them) is stored on your device so you can reopen them.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share or download a copy through your phone’s share sheet. Where it goes then is up to you.

What we don’t collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and it does not use your advertising ID. We don’t collect your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only when the content of your spreadsheet asks for it:

  • Picture links. If a cell contains a link to an image (for example https://…/photo.jpg), the app loads that image directly from the website it points to. That website can see the request, as with any image loaded on the web, and its own privacy policy applies.
  • Links you tap. Tapping a web address, email address or phone number in a row opens your browser, mail app or phone app. We don’t see what happens there.

Permissions

On Android the app requests only internet access. Files are opened through the system file picker, so the app sees only the files you choose.

Deleting your data

Remove a file from Recent from the ⋯ menu on the Files screen. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its stored copies, pictures and the recent-files list. Your original files are never changed or deleted by the app.

Children

The app is a general productivity tool and isn’t directed at children under 13. It doesn’t knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever adds features that change how data is handled (for example optional ads or in-app purchases), we will update this policy before that release and change the date at the top.
